How to Choose Your First Electric Scooter: A Beginner's Guide

📅 October 14, 2026 • Updated October 20 • ⏱️ 6 min read • ✍️ VoltRide Team • Guide

The market is full of models, and prices can range from a few thousand to more than ten thousand dirhams. Before comparing brands, answer five simple questions.

1. Range: How Many Kilometers Do You Actually Cover?

Calculate your daily distance (round trip if you can't charge at your destination):

  • Less than 10 km daily: An advertised range of ~25–30 km should suffice.
  • 10–20 km: Look for advertised range around 40 km or more.
  • More than 20 km: You need a large battery or ability to charge at work.
💡 Actual range is usually less than advertised, varying with your weight, hills, temperature, and speed. Calculate your approximate range in VoltRide before buying.

2. Speed: Mind the Law

According to Moroccan media reports on decree 2.25.145, the maximum speed for electric scooters is 25 km/h (15.5 mph). There's no point in spending more for a scooter that goes 45 or 60 km/h if you'll use it in the city. See our law article.

3. Weight: Can You Carry It?

  • 15 kg or less: Easy to carry on buses and public transport.
  • 15–25 kg: Better performance but relatively heavy.
  • More than 25 kg: Usually heavy-duty scooters with large motors, difficult to carry.

4. Battery: Voltage, Capacity, and Source

  • Energy in Wh = Voltage × Ampere-hours. This is the number that determines range, so compare between models.
  • Higher voltage usually means greater capacity, but it's not a quality indicator on its own.
  • Ask about cell type and battery warranty specifically.

5. After-Sales Service

  • Is there a service center or technician nearby?
  • Are replacement parts available (tire, brake, charger)?
  • Warranty: Duration and coverage (batteries usually have special conditions).
