Electric Scooter Accessories

A short guide to useful safety and everyday accessories. The links below open AliExpress search results, not verified seller listings. Check the exact product, specifications and seller before purchase.

What to check before buying

  • Choose a certified helmet that fits securely. Do not choose by appearance alone.
  • Match a lock to where you park and secure the frame to a fixed object.
  • Check light visibility, mounting security and weather resistance for your riding conditions.
  • Only buy a charger when its output voltage, connector, polarity and current match the scooter manufacturer's requirements.
