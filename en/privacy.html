Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

VoltRide is an independent project. Here we explain clearly what is stored and collected, and how you control it.

1. What stays on your device only

The app stores in your browser (localStorage) data that is not sent to us or to any server: your settings and language, the electricity tariff, your scooter's specifications, charge history, your logged trips and the reality factor calculated from them, Return Check inputs, favourites and comparison, and your cookie choice. We do not ask for an account, a name or an email to use the app.

2. Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We only load Google Analytics after your consent through the cookie banner. If you accept, statistical data may be collected, such as: the pages you visit, device type, browser and language, and an approximate location (country or city level). Google uses cookies such as _ga, which can last up to two years. We have turned off personalised advertising features and Google signals.

You can change your mind at any time: in the app under Settings → Analytics (cookies), or from the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of any page. If you decline, we stop measurement and delete our _ga cookies as far as possible.

3. Advertising and affiliate links

The site currently shows no advertising. If we add advertising or affiliate links in the future, we will update this page, ask for your consent to advertising cookies, and place a clear disclosure next to any paid link.

4. External links

Articles may contain links to other sites (for example official or news sources). We are not responsible for their policies.

5. Your rights

  • To delete the app's data from your device: Settings → Reset, or clear the site's data in your browser.
  • For any question or request about analytics data or your data in general: write to us at the address below.

6. Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the app's features change. Updates are published here and the "last updated" date is changed. This policy is governed by the laws in force in the Kingdom of Morocco.

8. Contact us

For questions: voltrideapp@gmail.com
