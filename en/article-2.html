Xiaomi or KuKirin? Specification Comparison

📅 October 14, 2026 • Updated October 20 • ⏱️ 6 min read • ✍️ VoltRide Team • Comparison

The two most common brands among electric scooter buyers in Morocco are Xiaomi (with Ninebot and others in the same category) and KuKirin. They differ in philosophy: Xiaomi focuses on lightness and simplicity, while KuKirin emphasizes power and range. This comparison is based on advertised specification ranges for both brands, not field tests.

⚠️ Legal Speed Limit in Morocco

According to Moroccan media reports on the 2.25.145 decree, the maximum allowed speed for electric scooters is 25 km/h (15.5 mph). Models capable of 45–70 km/h are practically designed for private use away from public roads, and those speeds don't mean you can use them in the city. Read our law article before buying.

🚴 Xiaomi: Light and Simple

Scooters of this category are lightweight and easy to fold, with speeds that comply with legal limits, making them suitable for daily city travel and beginners. Advantages: light weight, quick folding, smart app. Disadvantages: limited range on hills and heavy passenger weight.

⚡ KuKirin: Power and Range

Their models are stronger and heavier with larger motors and bigger batteries, suited for long-distance travel or those living in elevated areas. Advantages: greater range and power. Disadvantages: heavy weight makes carrying difficult, higher price, and advertised speeds may exceed legal limits for scooters.

🎯 How to Decide?

  • Can you carry your scooter on the bus or trains? Choose the lighter one.
  • Your daily distance more than 20 km or hilly terrain? Look for a larger battery.
  • Parts availability and service in your city: Ask the seller and experienced users before buying, as this varies from city to city.
