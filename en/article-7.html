Moroccan Scooter Law: What the Sources Confirm?

📅 October 15, 2026 • Updated November 12, 2026 • ⏱️ 6 min read • ✍️ VoltRide Team • Law

⚠️ Not Legal Advice

This article is a summary of what Moroccan media published, and we reviewed it after checking the sources below. Exact wording and details (definition, equipment, authentication, fines) must be verified in the official gazette decree or by relevant authorities.

📜 What is the Decree?

The decree number 2.25.145 amends and supplements Decree 2.10.420 concerning the application of the traffic code on roads. The decree is dated July 31, 2026 and was published in Official Gazette No. 7537 on August 24, 2026, starting on page 5444. The publication date and issue number are confirmed by the gazette index.

✅ What Sources Agree On

  • Helmet: Wearing a protective fastened helmet meeting certification requirements is mandatory.
  • Speed: Maximum 25 km/h. Some sources also state this vehicle category is defined between 6 and 25 km/h.
  • Headphones: Use of headphones while driving is prohibited for all cyclists and their vehicles.
  • Outside Cities: Riding outside built-up areas is allowed only on dedicated bike paths.

🟡 When Did These Rules Take Effect?

Decree 2.25.145 was published in Official Gazette No. 7537 on August 24, 2026, starting on page 5444. Reports differ in how they describe the six-month transition period. We have not verified the complete official texts of both decrees, so we cannot confidently attribute that period to either 2.25.145 or 2.24.393, or state which provisions it covers. We therefore avoid giving a definitive implementation date for that transition until the official texts are checked. Consult the Moroccan General Secretariat of Government’s Official Gazette archive and the contents and texts of Gazette No. 7537.

✅ What You Can Do Now

There's no need to wait: a helmet is useful for your safety today, avoid headphones, and stick to the 25 km/h limit. And if your scooter is faster, read our Beginner's Guide to Choosing Your Scooter and think about what's legal for you.

❓ Unconfirmed: We're Waiting for an Official Source

Some pages or comments included information we couldn't verify in reviewed sources, so we don't present them as facts:

  • Fines in dirhams: Sources mention fines fall under traffic code violation categories, without specifying amounts for these vehicles.
  • Specific mandatory equipment (lights, reflectors, horn, and dimensions) and technical inspection procedures for vehicles: we couldn't find confirmed details.
  • Insurance: Not covered by reviewed sources. Don't assume it's mandatory or not until you check with your insurance company or the official text.
  • Transporting scooters on trains and its fines: unconfirmed.

⚖️ Criminal Liability

A legal reading indicates the decree didn't create new crimes per se, but violating its rules may affect liability assessment in case of an accident covered by criminal law (such as non-intentional harm). Compliance with the helmet and speed limit protects you physically and legally.

🎯 Practical Summary

  1. Buy a good helmet and wear it always.
  2. Stay within 25 km/h limit on public roads.
  3. Don't wear headphones while riding.
  4. Don't leave the city by scooter except on dedicated bike paths.
  5. Follow official sources for remaining regulatory details.

We'll update this page when additional official information is released. Send us a correction from our contact page if you find an error.
