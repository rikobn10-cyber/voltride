Electric Scooter Accessories Buying Guide: What to Check Before Purchase

📅 October 26, 2026 • ⏱️ 7 min read • ✍️ VoltRide Team • Beginner Guide

The right accessory doesn't change your scooter—it protects you and your investment. This is a short buying guide: for each accessory, what to verify before you pay, not just a list of brand names. We may add affiliate links later, and will indicate them clearly. (Want a different format? Also see Our Best 5 Electric Scooter Accessories.)

⛑️ 1. Helmet: The Top Priority

Based on Moroccan press reports about Decree 2.25.145, wearing a attached protective helmet is mandatory for scooter riders. Regardless of the law, a helmet is the most important thing that protects you in a fall. For details on the legal requirements, see Our article on the Moroccan Law.

  • Choose a helmet certified with a recognized safety standard (e.g., CE marking for European bicycle helmets).
  • Try the fit: it shouldn't move on your head and should be tightened securely.
  • Replace it after any strong impact or if the shell or straps are damaged.

🔒 2. Strong Lock

  • Prefer a U-lock or reinforced chain. Thin cables can be cut easily.
  • Secure the wheel to a fixed object, not just the wheel alone.
  • Size should fit the places you usually stop, and weight shouldn't burden you.
  • A second lock in high-risk areas increases theft difficulty, but doesn't eliminate it.

💡 Lock it to a solid object, and if you can, don't leave it outside overnight. For understanding helmet legal requirements, see Moroccan Law Article.

3. Additional Lighting

The original lighting on some models is weak, especially for nighttime riding. The best is a strong forward light, red rear light, and side reflectors for visibility to other riders.

4. Bag or Phone Mount

Avoid carrying your belongings on your back or in your hands. A small bag on the handlebar for your phone and keys, and a stable phone mount. And be careful not to add weight to the handlebar which can affect steering.

5. Portable Air Pump

The right tire pressure helps preserve range and reduces puncture risk. A small portable pump (manual or electric) saves you on the road, and make sure it's compatible with your tire valve type.

Note

These are general recommendations. Some lighting and reflector/mirror requirements may appear in regulatory texts, so check official sources. See Our article on the Moroccan Law.
