Common Electric Scooter Error Codes

📅 October 16, 2026 • Updated October 20 • ⏱️ 5 min read • ✍️ VoltRide Team • Maintenance

⚠️ Important Before You Read

There is no unified error code format between companies. The same code may mean something different in Xiaomi, Ninebot, KuKirin, and others. What follows are general examples that are very common, and the manufacturer's manual for your specific model is the primary reference.

Seeing an error code on the screen doesn't necessarily mean a major fault. Many are caused by a loose connection or a dirty sensor. But when it comes to brakes and battery, don't take risks.

E14 (Example): Accelerometer Signal Issue

In some models, this indicates a fault in reading the accelerator lever or its wires.

✅ Respond: Turn the scooter off and restart it without touching the accelerator. Visually inspect any visible wires. If the error persists or acceleration is unpredictable, don't ride and take it to a technician.

E15 (Example): Brake Signal Issue

May appear when the brake lever is stuck or when there's a sensor fault.

✅ Respond: Ensure the lever returns freely and the cable isn't tight. If the error persists, seek inspection.

BAT (Example): Battery or Charging Alert

May indicate a severely low battery, a connection problem, a faulty charger, or an issue with the battery itself.

✅ Respond: Check you're using the correct charger and plug into a working outlet. If the battery heats up unusually or swells or smells, immediately stop charging, move the scooter away from flammable items, and seek expert help. Do not attempt to open it.

BRK (Example): Brake Warning

May indicate a fault in electronic brakes or wear in the brake disc.

✅ Respond: Do not continue riding if brake performance is weak. Inspect the brake disc and cables, and request an appropriate inspection.

🛡️ General Prevention

  • Inspect brakes and tires and wires at least once every two weeks.
  • Do not use pressurized water on electronic parts.
  • Store the scooter in a dry place.
  • Use the original or company-approved charger.

⚠️ Safety First

Do not open the battery or control panel unless you're qualified. The currents and voltages in electric scooters can cause burns or fires.
